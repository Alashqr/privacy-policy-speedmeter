Privacy Policy for SpeedMeter

📅 Last Updated: August 17, 2026
🇬🇧 English

1. Introduction

Welcome to SpeedMeter. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the confidentiality of your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our application.

2. Data Collection

SpeedMeter does NOT collect any personal data from users. All operations and processing are performed locally on your device only.

✅ We DO NOT:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Track geographic location
  • Store any data on external servers
  • Share data with third parties

3. Data Accessed

The app accesses the following data only to display speed in the status bar:

  • Network State: To check internet connectivity (Wi-Fi or cellular data).
  • Traffic Statistics (TrafficStats): To calculate download and upload speeds. This data is not stored and is used only for real-time display.
  • Notifications: To display internet speed in the status bar. The app requires notification permission on Android 13+.

4. Permissions Required

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display the meter in the notification bar (Android 13+).
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To check internet connectivity status.

5. Data Sharing

No data is shared with any third party. The app does not use any analytics services or advertisements.

6. Data Security

Since the app does not collect or store any data, the risk of hacking or data leakage is zero. All data is processed instantly and discarded after display.

7. User Rights

  • You may uninstall the app at any time.
  • You may revoke app permissions from your device settings.
  • All data is stored locally and deleted upon uninstallation.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You will be notified of any material changes through app updates or via this page.
